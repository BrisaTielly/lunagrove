# Lunagrove Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 30, 2026_

Lunagrove is a Pomodoro focus timer that runs entirely inside your browser.

## What Lunagrove stores

Lunagrove saves the following in Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`), on your device only:

- your timer preferences (focus and break durations, sounds, notifications, motion);
- the state of the current session;
- your focus history: how many sessions and minutes you completed each day, used for the garden and the stats screen.

## What Lunagrove does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data.
- It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no remote servers.
- It does not read, modify, or access any website or browsing activity.
- It does not load or execute remote code.

## Backups

The "Export backup" button creates a JSON file on your computer with the data listed above. It never leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling Lunagrove deletes everything it stored. You can also restore a backup at any time from the settings.
